Node War: panel language

Every panel is built from one recipe, taken from the player card you like: a parchment fill, a 2 px ink outline, and a thicker bottom edge in place of a shadow. Display text is Lilita One; body text is Fredoka SemiBold. All text gets extra letter spacing, so it reads lighter without losing weight. The 3D world stays visible: pages have no backdrop panel, and only content sits on cards.

4. Item sheet: dots inside the window, skins row

The one recipe

  • Parchment fill, 2 px ink outline, a thicker bottom edge as the only shadow (5 px for panels, 3 to 5 px for rows and buttons).
  • Corners: 14 px for panels, 12 px for fields, 8 to 10 px for buttons and avatars.
  • Three edge colours. Outer (ink #2d2319) for panels that sit on the world. Inner (#4d3d2d, close but a little lighter) for anything outlined inside a panel. Light (#c3aa85) only for a lip that sits inside a shaded region, such as the selected face of a switch.
  • Pressing a button collapses its bottom edge, so it physically goes down.
  • Subtle paper grain on every panel; no blur, no glass.

One outline level

  • Never put an outlined pill inside an outlined box. Inside a panel, things are flat: filled, sunk or divided by hairlines.
  • Lists are a ledger: one card, a darker header strip, flat 56 px rows. This replaces the thin name panels.
  • The only outlined things inside a panel are buttons, because they are pressable.

Type

  • Lilita One for names, numbers, titles and buttons. Fredoka SemiBold for body text and meta lines.
  • All text is tracked out 0.04 em: lighter to read, same weight.
  • Stat histograms are 10 cells and fill the sheet width.
  • Sizes: title 34, panel heading 17 to 24, name 15 to 17, body 15 to 16, meta 13. Nothing below 13.
  • Page titles are stamped straight onto the world (ink stroke and drop), with no panel behind them.

Space

  • 4/8 scale: 16 screen edge, 8 between cards, 12 inside panels, 12 between a row's avatar and text.
  • Rows are 56 px; live rows are 48 px cards with a 58 px VS, so the VS stands proud of both cards.
  • Name on top, RR tight underneath (12 px) inside the 48 px card, so there is no empty band above names.

The world shows through

  • Pages have no full-screen backdrop. Cards hold content; the 3D scene fills the gaps.
  • Scrims are light (25%), so the world stays visible behind sheets and bubbles.
  • The Shop gets its own language later; it is not part of this sheet.

2 to 3 option switches

  • The outer box has the outline and the ink lip.
  • The shaded inner track has no lip.
  • The selected face is lighter, with a weaker paper-coloured lip. It sits 4 px inside the track, so the outer border always shows in full.
  • A switch lives in the panel it filters (Owned/All sits with the cards).

Pill nav

  • Flat: the selected tab is a gold face with an ink outline. No lip and no raise.

Don't

OwnedAllSort
Outlined pills inside an outlined box: the old Workshop filters.

Icon library options

The same 14 jobs in each free library: menu, bell, search, filter, shop, workshop, home, battle, watch, guild, add friend, coins, settings and refresh. Ink icons on parchment, then the top-bar style (light on dark).
